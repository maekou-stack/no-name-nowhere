About

Koh Maeda is a bilingual creator and designer working at the intersection of media, fashion, and AI. Based in San Francisco, he builds across formats — broadcast, brand, and digital — fluent in both English and Japanese.

His work explores the dialogue between tradition and innovation, rooted in Japanese craft sensibility and reimagined for a contemporary global context.

Philosophy

made with hands.
think with heart.

In a world of speed and mass production, the work chooses to be made slowly and intentionally — to honor materials, to respect stories, and to leave space for meaning.

Process

Research Exploring culture, materials, and personal narratives.
Concept Building a clear view, with emotion and concrete depth.
Experiment Prototyping across mediums — cloth, code, frame, screen.
Craft Crafting with intention, clarity, and care.
Share Bringing the work into the world to connect and resonate.
